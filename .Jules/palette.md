## 2026-08-03 - Fix unnatural upward translation on active states\n**Learning:** In CSS, negative `translateY` values move elements upwards. While hover states often use upward motion to indicate interactivity, applying it to `:active` (mouse down) states breaks the physical metaphor of pushing a button. Users expect clicked elements to travel downwards or inwards.\n**Action:** Always ensure `:active` interactive states use positive `translateY` values (e.g., `translateY(1px)`) or scaling (e.g., `scale(0.98)`) to correctly mimic a tactile physical press.
