## 2025-07-29 - Improve Interactive Card Active States
**Learning:** Users found the default `:active` state of interactive cards (moving upwards with `translateY(-4px)` or `translateY(-2px)`) jarring, as it conflicts with the physical intuition of pressing a button (which should compress or move downwards).
**Action:** Changed the `:active` states for `.quickLinkCard` and `.contractCard` in `src/pages/index.module.css` to use a downward translation (`translateY(1px)`). This establishes a reusable pattern for interactive elements where the "press" interaction mimics a physical button press by moving down instead of up.
